What the platform does

Built around the Manager.io invoicing workflow

Process Nigeria e-invoices while continuing to use your normal Manager.io accounting workflow.

01

Sales Invoice Processing

Validate, sign and submit Manager.io Sales Invoices to the configured MBS/NRS environment.

02

Credit Notes

Process Credit Notes with verified linkage to the original submitted invoice and its NRS IRN.

03

TEST & PRODUCTION

Server-locked TEST and PRODUCTION routes keep sandbox processing separate from official production records.

04

NRS IRN & QR Code

Write the issued NRS IRN and encrypted QR image back into Manager.io for customer-facing documents.

05

Payment Status Sync

Synchronise final payment status after Manager.io confirms that the invoice closing balance has reached zero.

06

Business-Scoped Security

Each Manager.io business uses its own tenant record, encrypted credentials, subscription and processing history.

Installation

Start from one Manager.io Custom Button

The bootstrap button launches the guided setup. The installer then creates or repairs the required integration components.

Manager.io Bootstrap Setup

Add this URL to the initial Nigeria E-Invoicing Custom Button.

Bootstrap / Installation URL
https://einvoice.adib.com.ng/extension/invoice

After opening the bootstrap button from inside Manager.io, choose Install Nigeria E-Invoicing and follow the guided registration process.

TEST and PRODUCTION extension routes are designed to be launched from inside Manager.io so the current business and document context can be detected correctly.

Frequently asked questions

Common onboarding questions

Do TEST transactions affect official PRODUCTION records?

No. The application uses separate server-locked TEST and PRODUCTION routes with separate environment credentials and Manager.io operational fields.

How many free submissions are included?

A newly registered business receives 10 successful trial submissions. Failed validations or status checks do not count as successful submissions.

Can Production be configured during the trial?

Yes. A customer may configure, test and activate the separate Production profile while trial access is still available.

What appears on the customer invoice?

The standard customer-facing e-invoicing print profile is designed to expose the NRS IRN and NRS QR Code while keeping operational status fields hidden.

How does annual payment work?

Annual access is paid through the configured Selar checkout. After payment is verified, the tenant continues under the 365-day annual plan.

Can I start without Production credentials?

Yes. The business may begin with TEST configuration and trial processing first. The separate PRODUCTION profile can be configured when the business is ready to go live.
